## What Ledge is today

An ADHD-first sidebar notepad for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. Plain Markdown in the user's own iCloud Drive folder, no accounts, no telemetry, no server. Shared `LedgeCore` Swift package (24 unit tests), AppKit Mac app built by `scripts/build-mac.sh` (raw swiftc), SwiftUI iOS and watchOS apps generated by XcodeGen from `apps/ios/project.yml`, WidgetKit extension (iOS 18), plus a hand-built Shortcuts capture net (`shortcuts/README.md`).

## What was broken: the Siri capture regression

Symptom: saying a capture phrase to Siri no longer files a thought into Ledge.

Diagnosis, grounded in the built product and the written record:

1. **The phrase you say was never registered.** The built app's `Metadata.appintents` (inspected this session) registered exactly two Siri phrases: "Add to Ledge" and "Capture to Ledge". "Capture my thought" and every natural variant matched nothing, on any build that ever shipped.
2. **Three actions shared one name.** The app's spool-writing `CaptureIntent` is titled "Capture to Ledge". The widget extension's open-the-app `LaunchCaptureIntent` was also titled "Capture to Ledge" and was discoverable. The hand-built Shortcuts recipe (shortcuts/README.md) is also named "Capture to Ledge". Siri gives a personal shortcut priority over an App Shortcut phrase with the same name, so the phrase most likely ran the recipe, not the intent.
3. **The recipe it ran has a documented silent failure.** Before v0.3.1, every app spool drain replaced `drop.md` atomically and orphaned the recipe's file bookmark; commit `234ff67` and shortcuts/README.md record that the recipe then "failed on every run" until manually re-pointed at the file. The fix stopped future orphaning but could not heal an already-broken bookmark, and no artifact records the heal ever happening. This is the most probable moment "Siri capture worked" became "Siri capture is broken": between the iOS app install (2026-07-24) and the fix (2026-07-27), with the breakage persisting on-device ever since.
4. Ruled out by direct evidence: AppShortcutsProvider phrase syntax (both phrases carry the applicationName token), build-time metadata extraction (verified written, with NLU assets, in this session's build), missing openAppWhenRun (deliberately false, correct for a background intent), Info.plist and entitlement problems (zero-entitlement design, builds clean), and a data-layer failure inside the intent (SpoolWriter falls back to a local pending queue rather than failing).
5. Not provable from this Mac: the exact state of the iPhone (installed build, folder bookmark validity, contents of the local pending queue, the personal shortcut's current name and health). The test script below distinguishes the remaining cases.

### The fix (commit `4a26a6e`)

- `CaptureIntent.swift`: seven phrases now registered, including "Capture my thought in Ledge", "Capture a thought in Ledge", and "Ledge, capture my thought", plus a shortTitle and system image so the shortcut surfaces properly in Spotlight and the Shortcuts app.
- `LedgeWidgets.swift`: the opener intent retitled "Open Ledge Capture" and hidden from discovery (`isDiscoverable = false`). The Control Center button is unaffected. The name "Capture to Ledge" now belongs to exactly one action.
- `PanelContent.swift` (Mac): the 2-second heartbeat now checks the spool as well as inbox.md (parity with iOS commit `a707685`), so an out-of-app capture folds in while the panel is open.
- Verified: 24 core tests pass, Mac build succeeds, iOS simulator build succeeds, and the rebuilt `Metadata.appintents` contains all seven phrases and the retitled, hidden widget intent.

### Manual test script (iPhone, physical device)

1. On the iPhone, open Shortcuts and look for a personal shortcut named "Capture to Ledge". Rename it to "Ledge fallback capture" (or delete it if you never healed its file bookmark). This removes the name collision permanently.
2. Connect the iPhone (cable or same Wi-Fi), then from the repo: `./scripts/deploy.sh` (or `cd apps/ios && xcodegen generate` then deploy from Xcode). Launch Ledge once by hand after install; iOS registers App Shortcut phrases at first launch.
3. Say: **"Hey Siri, capture my thought in Ledge."** Expected: Siri asks "What's the thought?", you dictate, Siri replies "Captured." Also test "Capture to Ledge" and "Add to Ledge".
4. Open the Ledge app on the phone. Expected: the thought appears in the inbox within a second or two (scene activation drains the spool and flushes any pending queue). If old missing thoughts suddenly appear here, they were sitting in the on-device pending queue, which means the folder bookmark had broken; re-pick the folder in the app if it prompts.
5. On the Mac, summon the panel (Option+Space). Expected: the dictated thought is present, tagged `· iPhone`, along with the capture from 2026-08-16 that was stranded in the spool.
6. If the phrase is not recognized at step 3: reboot the phone (Siri re-indexes App Shortcuts), confirm Settings > Apps > Ledge > Siri is enabled, and check the app appears in Shortcuts under "Capture" with the new phrases listed. To watch resolution live, connect the phone to the Mac, open Console.app against the device, filter process `assistantd` (or search "CaptureIntent"), and say the phrase.
